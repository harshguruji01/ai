About GuruAI & HarshGuruJi

Empowering developers, students, and creators with lightning-fast AI reasoning and image generation.

Our Mission

GuruAI was conceptualized and built by HarshGuruJi (Harsh Patel) with a focused mission: to deliver accessible, high-speed, multimodal artificial intelligence without unnecessary complexity or paywalls. Accessible on both web (ai.webguruji.online) and Android native mobile app, GuruAI bridges cutting-edge reasoning with practical developer and creative workflows.

Core Technology & Capabilities

🧠 Guru-Flash 1.1 Architecture

High-speed conversational reasoning engine engineered for deep coding analysis, multilingual Hindi/Hinglish dialog, and instantaneous answers.
